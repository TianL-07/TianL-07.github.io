Research background

Single cells, immune repertoires

My research background in single-cell and immune-repertoire analysis, and the questions that draw me to computational immunology.

23 Oct 20261 min read

I study Biomedical Informatics at Zhejiang University. My research experience includes single-cell RNA sequencing and immune-repertoire analysis.

I am interested in how computational approaches can help us understand the immune system, from the states of individual cells to the diversity of their receptors.

Research interests

  • Single-cell analysis and cellular heterogeneity
  • B-cell and T-cell receptor repertoires
  • Computational approaches to immune recognition

This page is a brief introduction to my research interests. Project summaries and shareable results will be added as they are ready.
